Pre-Tinkering Lab

A playful introduction to STEAM for primary students, built around six hands-on kits.

Designed for Classes 2–5, the Pre-Tinkering Lab uses safe, age-appropriate kits and guided activities to build early analytical thinking and creativity before students move on to full tinkering or robotics labs.

Key Features

  • Six integrated curriculum kits
  • Safe, age-appropriate equipment
  • Blends structured content with playful exploration
  • Recommended lab space: 500–1,200 sq. ft.
