Atal Tinkering Lab

End-to-end setup of the Atal Innovation Mission's flagship ATL programme, run by NITI Aayog.

Atal Tinkering Labs are a Government of India initiative under the Atal Innovation Mission to build scientific temperament and innovation skills. We deliver all four GEM-certified equipment packages plus curriculum, training and LMS access.

Key Features

  • Package 1: electronics, robotics, IoT & sensors
  • Package 2: rapid prototyping tools (incl. 3D printers)
  • Package 3: mechanical, electrical & measurement tools
  • Package 4: power supply, accessories & safety equipment
