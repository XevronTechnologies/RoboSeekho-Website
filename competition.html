About RoboSeekho

About the Competition

The RoboSeekho National Innovation Challenge 2026 is a nationwide innovation competition designed for students to showcase creative technological solutions for real-world problems.

We believe in the power of young minds to create a smarter, safer and more innovative tomorrow. This competition is a platform for students to learn, build, innovate and make a real impact.
